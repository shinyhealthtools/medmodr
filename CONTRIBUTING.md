## Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive. Any form of discrimination or toxic behavior will not be tolerated.

---

## How to Contribute

You can contribute in many ways:

- 🐛 **Report bugs** — Open an issue with clear reproduction steps.
- 💡 **Suggest features** — Tell us what statistical tools you need.
- 📝 **Improve docs** — Fix typos or clarify usage examples.
- 💻 **Write code** — Fix issues or add new features.
- 🌍 **Translate** — Help localize MedModr for non-English users.

---

## Reporting Bugs

If you find a bug (e.g., wrong p-value, UI breakage, calculation error):

1. **Search existing issues** to avoid duplicates.
2. Open a **New Issue** with the label `bug`.
3. Include:
   - **Browser version** (Chrome 120, Firefox 121, etc.)
   - **Steps to reproduce**
   - **Expected vs actual result**
   - **Screenshot or console error** (if applicable)
